Rex Hermes

A private personal assistant used only by its owner to read and organise their own Gmail and Google Calendar.

Privacy Policy

This app is used solely by its owner. Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide features to the owner, is not sold, and is not shared with any third party. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
